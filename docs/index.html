Frequently asked questions

Does Bloo need an internet connection?
No. Bloo works fully offline. All your data stays on your device.

Does Bloo collect my data?
No. Bloo does not collect, track, or share any personal data.

Can I use Bloo in Turkish?
Yes. Bloo supports both English and Turkish. You can switch the language from Settings.

Privacy

Bloo does not collect any personal data. Everything you enter (habits, progress, your Bloo's name) is stored only on your device.
