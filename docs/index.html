FAQ

Does Bloo need an internet connection?

No. Bloo works fully offline and all your data stays on your device.

Does Bloo collect my data?

No. Bloo does not collect, track, or share any personal data.

Is Bloo available in Turkish?

Yes. Bloo supports English and Turkish. Switch languages anytime from Settings.

What happens if I miss a day?

Nothing bad. Your Bloo waits for you, just pick back up whenever you're ready.

Privacy

Bloo does not collect any personal data. Everything you enter, your habits, your progress, your Bloo's name, is stored only on your device and never leaves it.
